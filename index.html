Why tokenized funds,
and why Canton.

Tokenized money-market funds and Treasuries on Canton can settle at any hour, on any day. That is what lets an agent work the evening and the weekend.

Cash and fund units change hands in one atomic transaction. There is no gap where you have paid and not yet received.

Canton is a public network built for finance, with privacy at its core. Your positions and movements are visible only to you and the counterparties to each transaction.

Avessa’s workflows are written as Daml smart contracts. Who may move what, and within which limits, is defined in the transaction itself.

Questions a careful CFO asks.

If yours isn’t here, request access and ask us directly.

Is Avessa a bank or a fund?

No. Avessa is a technology company. It is not a bank, a broker-dealer or an investment adviser, and it does not run a fund. The agent moves your cash between your own accounts and the instruments your policy allows.

What does the agent put our cash into?

Only what your policy permits: tokenized money-market funds and tokenized Treasury bills. Your CFO chooses the eligible instruments and the limit for each. Nothing else is ever bought.

What return should we expect?

We don’t quote one. What your cash earns depends on the instruments you choose and on market rates at the time. Avessa’s job is narrower: cut the hours your cash spends doing nothing, without putting a payment at risk.

What if we need the money sooner than planned?

Your minimum operating balance stays at the bank at all times. For anything unscheduled, an authorised person can recall cash from the dashboard. Because settlement on Canton is available around the clock, a recall doesn’t have to wait for a business day.

Can the agent act outside our rules?

No. It acts only within the policy your CFO approves. Moves above your threshold wait for a named approver, every action is logged, and you can pause the agent at any time.

Who can see our positions?

On Canton, positions and movements are visible only to the parties involved in them. Your balances are not published for the market to read.
